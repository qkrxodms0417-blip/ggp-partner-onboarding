9 To 광고주 · 전환 태그 데이터와 개인정보 기준 안내 (영문)

글로벌 광고주가 IP 주소 · 쿠키 식별자를 개인정보로 분류해 DDQ 보완이나 DPA를 요청할 때, 네이버 기준을 먼저 설명하는 용도입니다. 네이버 공개 정책 문구를 옮긴 것이며, 실사 답변은 담당 AM을 거쳐 네이버가 작성합니다.

EN · 파트너사 → 해외 광고주 
Dear [Name],

Thank you for your questions on how the NAVER conversion tag handles data. Please find NAVER's position below.

1. What is collected
The NAVER conversion tag (//wcs.naver.net/wcslog.js) runs in the visitor's browser and sends the page URL, HTTP referrer, visit time, cookies and browsing environment (browser, screen resolution, OS). The IP address is transmitted automatically as part of TCP/IP. The cookie identifier is a random value issued per browser by NAVER and is not linked to your membership data. Directly identifying information (name, email, phone number, national ID) is not collected.

2. How NAVER classifies this data
Under the Korean Personal Information Protection Act, this data is treated as behavioral information. NAVER stores it separately from member information and never combines the two, so it is not processed as personal information.
- NAVER Privacy Policy, Section 11 (Processing of Behavioral Information): https://policy.naver.com/policy/privacy.html
- NAVER Personalized Advertising Notice, Section 6: NAVER does not combine browser cookies or advertising identifiers with personal information. https://gam.naver.com/optout/main

3. Why a DPA does not apply
NAVER collects this data directly from users' browsers and processes it under its own advertising service standards, as a common service offered to all advertisers. NAVER does not process data on your behalf as a processor, so an individual DPA is not applicable.
- NAVER Ads Operation Policy, Article 11: https://ads.naver.com/adguide/policy

4. Retention and location
Raw reporting data is kept for 7 days and then only as aggregated statistics. Conversion tag data used for measurement and targeting is deleted after a maximum of 180 days. Data is stored and processed in NAVER data centers in Korea.

We understand your internal standards may classify IP addresses and cookie identifiers as personal data. We hope this explains how NAVER handles them. Please let us know if you need anything further.

Best regards,
